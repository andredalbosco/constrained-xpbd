# Which choices have actually been composed?

The choices compose, but evidence belongs to a complete combination of objective, work target, constraints, solver, and numerical tolerances. A successful ingredient in one row has not automatically been validated in another. The table distinguishes trajectory experiments from algebraic checks; “physical grid” means a mechanical mesh with cycles, not a parameter sweep.

## Minimal next cross-experiment

1. **Preserve one complete objective.** Use the quarter independent-v objective, frozen start-only midpoint-DG work target, and explicit component energy/work scales. On one compliant chain, one small cyclic mesh, and two independent objects sharing only a stationary pin, compare full W/KKT with reduced work. Require matching objective, reconstructed balances/multipliers, stationary root and exact reduced curvature before comparing cost.
2. **Transfer one numerical choice at a time.** Keep dense GN-then-Newton as the oracle. Integrate the verified graph operator into the reduced derivatives, then derive block/graph factors for that constrained system. Compare with the same full objective solved by stable local AL. Recheck raw residuals: neither the existing DG preconditioner nor a smaller KKT matrix establishes equivalence, convergence, or speed here.
3. **Add the rigid composition separately.** First combine full explicit work with the old-reaction predictor, endpoint geometry and tangency on a double pendulum; only then test reduced work and a mixed rigid/compliant fixture. Retain canonical and RATTLE controls. Gate conclusions on completed time, phase/modal transfer, component energy, curvature and setup-inclusive cost—not energy alone.

These are proposed cross-experiments. The repository adapter, arbitrary 3D meshes, sparse mechanical graph operator, mixed rigidity, and production defaults remain unimplemented; see [integration proposal](INTEGRATION.md) and [hour findings](RESULTS.md).
